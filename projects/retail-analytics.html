01 The Business Problem: Silent Revenue Erosion

An e-commerce retail enterprise recorded strong top-line sales of ৳1.94M across 70 orders and 10 nationwide city hubs. However, the executive committee noticed that cash flow did not match reported bookings. Customer acquisition spending was surging, but order fulfillment was suffering from silent bottlenecks.

The Diagnostic Goal: Write robust, production SQL queries with zero autocomplete or AI assistance to answer 4 critical business questions:

  • Exactly how much revenue was lost to cancelled orders, and what portion remains trapped in returns?
  • Which customer accounts registered but never placed a single order (first-touch churn)?
  • How does high-value customer lifetime spend compare against the overall company average?
  • Which product categories drive volume versus margin across each geographic district?

02 Relational Data Architecture (3NF SQLite Schema)

The audit was conducted on a normalized 4-table relational database model (retail_analytics.db) enforcing referential integrity between customer profiles, order headers, line items, and product masters:

customers (25 rows) Dimension
  • PK customer_id (INTEGER)
  • customer_name (TEXT)
  • city (TEXT)
  • signup_date (TEXT)
orders (70 rows) Fact Header
  • PK order_id (INTEGER)

03 Production SQL Query Architecture

Below are the exact SQL patterns engineered to extract insights without intermediate application-layer overhead:

1. Anti-Join: Detecting Zero-Order Dormant Customer Churn

Result: 5 Dormant Accounts Identified 
-- Isolates registered users who have NEVER completed a transaction
SELECT 
    c.customer_id,
    c.customer_name,
    c.city,
    c.signup_date
FROM customers c
LEFT JOIN orders o ON c.customer_id = o.customer_id
WHERE o.order_id IS NULL;

2. Chained CTE: Customer Value Tiering (Platinum, Gold, Silver)

Logic: Multi-Level Aggregation 
WITH CustomerTotals AS (
    SELECT 
        c.customer_id,
        c.customer_name,
        c.city,
        COUNT(o.order_id) AS total_orders,
        COALESCE(SUM(o.total_amount), 0) AS lifetime_spend
    FROM customers c
    LEFT JOIN orders o ON c.customer_id = o.customer_id 
        AND o.order_status = 'Delivered'
    GROUP BY c.customer_id, c.customer_name, c.city
),
TierSegmented AS (
    SELECT 
        *,
        CASE 
            WHEN lifetime_spend >= 100000 THEN 'Platinum'
            WHEN lifetime_spend >= 50000  THEN 'Gold'
            WHEN lifetime_spend > 0       THEN 'Silver'
            ELSE 'Dormant'
        END AS customer_tier
    FROM CustomerTotals
)
SELECT customer_tier, COUNT(*) AS customer_count, SUM(lifetime_spend) AS tier_revenue
FROM TierSegmented
GROUP BY customer_tier
ORDER BY tier_revenue DESC;

3. Window Function: Top 2 Best-Selling Products per Category

Logic: PARTITION BY + DENSE_RANK 
WITH CategoryRanked AS (
    SELECT 
        p.category,
        p.product_name,
        SUM(oi.quantity) AS total_units_sold,
        SUM(oi.quantity * oi.unit_price) AS total_revenue,
        DENSE_RANK() OVER (
            PARTITION BY p.category 
            ORDER BY SUM(oi.quantity) DESC
        ) AS rank_in_category
    FROM order_items oi
    JOIN products p ON oi.product_id = p.product_id
    JOIN orders o ON oi.order_id = o.order_id
    WHERE o.order_status != 'Cancelled'
    GROUP BY p.category, p.product_name
)
SELECT * FROM CategoryRanked
WHERE rank_in_category <= 2;

04 Analytical Report & Key Forensic Discoveries

01

৳ 449,050 Trapped Margin

02

20% Immediate Onboarding Drop

The anti-join revealed that 5 out of 25 registered customers never completed their first order. Marketing spent acquisition budget on these users with zero downstream lifetime value realized.

03

The "Mymensingh Paradox"

Dhaka volume averaged an AOV of only ৳20,833, whereas tier-2 cities dominated high-ticket purchasing: Mymensingh recorded an AOV of ৳47,650 (2.3x Dhaka) and Gazipur recorded ৳37,528.

05 Actionable Recommendations & ROI

1

Automated First-Purchase Nudge Engine

Deploy an automated event trigger for accounts with 0 orders after 72 hours of signup. Offering a targeted 5% first-order discount would reactivate at least 2 of the 5 dormant accounts, unlocking an estimated ৳60K+ in initial gross margin.

2

Pre-Fulfillment Quality Checks on High-Ticket Electronics

Electronics drives ৳1.16M (60% of total revenue). Instituting a pre-dispatch verification protocol would cut the ৳361K return rate by at least 35%, preserving approx ৳126K in logistics overhead.

3

Reallocate Marketing Budget to Tier-2 High-AOV Hubs

Shift 25% of digital ad spend from low-AOV Dhaka (৳20.8K) to high-intent regional hubs like Mymensingh (৳47.6K), Gazipur (৳37.5K), and Rangpur (৳33.4K).
